== Description ==

**Finally, A/B Testing & Personalization Built for WordPress**

You spend hours creating content, but are you sure it's working? Every headline, button, and image you publish is a guess. Wasted traffic and lost sales are the cost of guessing wrong.

Accelerate eliminates the guesswork. It’s the native CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) platform that puts the power of A/B testing and personalization directly inside the WordPress Block Editor. No more expensive SaaS subscriptions. No more complex integrations. Just data-driven results.

While Accelerate is still in beta, we have released the following features.

### What Can You Do With Accelerate?

- **Run A/B Tests on Any Block:** Create multiple versions of your headlines, calls-to-action, or images and let Accelerate tell you which one performs best. Know exactly what works.
- **Personalize Content for Different Audiences:**  Show unique content to visitors based on where they came from, their on-site behavior, and more. Deliver the perfect message every time.
- **Understand Your Content with Block-Level Analytics:**  See exactly how people interact with your site on a block-by-block basis. Our GDPR-compliant analytics gives you actionable insights you own completely.
- **Promote Content Site-Wide with Broadcast Blocks:**  Instantly advertise new offers or important announcements across your entire site from one central location.
- **Manage Everything from a Marketer's Dashboard:**  Our Content Explorer gives you a single, unified view of your content performance.

### Built for the Modern WordPress Creator

Accelerate is designed from the ground up to be seamless. It integrates directly with the tools you already use, including Synced Patterns (Reusable Blocks), to supercharge your workflow.

### Important Beta Information:

Accelerate is in active development (beta). We invite you to be one of our first users and help shape the future of WordPress optimization. To power our analytics engine, a free account registration is required upon installation. All data is securely hosted in the EU and is 100% GDPR compliant.

### FAQ

**How do I use Accelerate?**
Please refer to our [User Guide for Accelerate](https://altis-dxp.notion.site/Altis-Accelerate-Guide-90581042329742538523ebddb41f1d4f) during our beta phase.

**How is the analytics data hosted?**
During this beta phase, all data is stored in the EU (specifically in Frankfurt, Germany). We may offer other jurisdictions in the future — please let us know if you have any requests with regards to jurisdictions .Data is never shared with third parties, or transferred out of the region. Everything is hosted at AWS and automatically deleted after 90 days.

All data collected by Accelerate is subject to the European GDPR regulations, as processed by Human Made Ltd, a UK registered company.

Accelerate is business-to-business software, and contractual terms including consent may apply per your contract. We automatically collect business-level usage data under the terms of these contractual agreements, and you can choose to opt-in to providing personal data to help us make our product better.

Data about your visitors is automatically collected by Accelerate to power analytics and personalisation features, based on the visitor’s opt-in status.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Why do I need an Accelerate subscription (free)? =

Accelerate automatically collects and processes analytics data using the our analytics service. A connection to this paid service is required in order to collect and process this data.

= Where did Reusable blocks go? =

WordPress core transitioned to the "Synced Pattern" naming convention in 6.3. Accelerate encourages thinking with a reusable content mindset.

= Where did the AI feature go? =

The AI features were experimental while Accelerate was in beta, but we have taken the decision to remove it in favour of keeping the plugin focused on marketing and analytics. We may bring those features to a standalone plugin in the future.
